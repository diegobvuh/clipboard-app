## Users
The first user is the owner's Depop shop. The shop is currently idle with approximately 30 one-of-a-kind thrifted garments awaiting listing; the tedious work after taking photographs is the main pain point. Existing photographs include brand, size, and other tags. The intended future audience is Depop sellers subscribing to the product.

## Product Purpose
Clipboard helps sellers prepare garment listings and ultimately manage more of a resale business with an AI assistant. The first release prioritizes turning garment photos into listing drafts with as little extra work as possible. Over time, the owner wants Clipboard to become the dashboard for a broader clothing business and brand, including original clothing and secondhand curation. Current planning and the initial product remain focused on Depop.

## Operating Context
Sellers capture both listing photos and close-ups of garment details and flaws. AI creates descriptions for review. The longer-term vision includes scheduling, buyer communications, social promotion, and detailed analytics with AI feedback.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The long-term goal is a conversational AI employee that learns the business and can eventually work autonomously. AI autonomy should be configurable for each business. The assistant should adapt to approved and rejected responses and maintain business-specific context. The owner wants an AI-controlled computer to perform Depop work; this approach carries Depop terms and account risks and is not confirmed authorized. Depop integration access has not been obtained. The extent of supported posting, messaging, and other automation remains to be verified with Depop. The seller chooses public photos per listing. AI should research and suggest strategic prices, category, brand, size, and persuasive, accurate descriptions, with seller review. Pricing should balance profit and reasonable sales velocity. The owner wants to avoid leaving potential profit on the table; research quality and source access must be visible. Independence should increase gradually through review and feedback. Inventory should track each item’s purchase cost. During live thrifting sessions the seller photographs all candidate garments and their price tags, tracks estimated spending, discards items not bought, and keeps purchased items. Receipts are a trip spending record, not a reliable item identifier. The assistant should support the seller during the session and suggest accurate handling of tax, discounts, and other expenses. A finance section is desired; continuous live camera support is a possible later feature. Measurement practices are configurable by business; the owner generally measures when customers ask. Native SwiftUI is recommended; the final stack and review workflow remain open decisions.

## Brand Commitments
Clipboard is a working title. The interface should feel as minimal, simple, and usable as Depop.

## Evidence on Hand
No actual shop data, metrics, product photographs, or integrations are available in the project yet.

## Product Principles
- Make photographing and drafting listings fast and straightforward.
- Make garment condition clear and reviewable.
- Let each business choose and understand its AI assistant's authority.
- Adapt to feedback while keeping seller control visible.
